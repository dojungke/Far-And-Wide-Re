# Character Hand Art Rules

These rules are mandatory for every new or edited character hand, paw, hand sprite, card-holding pose, icon, and generated character-art asset in this project.

- Hands are anthropomorphic feline forepaws, never realistic human hands.
- Each hand has exactly four digits: one short opposable thumb and three short rounded fingers.
- The thumb is a small, rounded side toe. It must be functional for gripping, but never long, sharply separated, or human-like.
- Keep the complete silhouette broad, short, soft, and paw-like. The wrist must flow naturally into the paw.
- Hand-back views use only minimal toe-separation lines and show no paw pads.
- Palm views show exactly five matte light-pink pads: one large central pad plus four smaller digit pads, including one on the thumb.
- Do not use five fingers, long fingers, visible joints, realistic creases, veins, human nails, or long sharp claws.

Use this fixed image-generation direction when relevant:
