# Character Card Art Style Rules

Apply these rules to every newly generated or edited character-card illustration.

- The supplied white feline character sheet is the strict primary style reference.
- Faithfully match its compact chibi proportions, fluffy rounded white bob, large simple turquoise oval eyes, pale-pink ear interiors, thin tidy near-black outlines, soft minimal white/cream cel shading, oversized white robe, and teal bow with long ribbon ends.
- Full-body framing is optional. Prefer close-up, waist-up, or action-focused framing whenever it preserves the reference style more accurately.
- Do not use a glossy, highly rendered, thick-lined, generic anime, or otherwise different art style.
- All card illustrations use 1536x1264px (1.215:1).
- PNG assets must have genuine alpha transparency; a checkerboard pattern is never an acceptable substitute.
- Follow the project's feline-paw rule: each visible hand has exactly four digits, one short rounded thumb and three short rounded fingers.
